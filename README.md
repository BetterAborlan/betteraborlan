## What this is

BetterAborlan.org is a volunteer-built website that brings the Municipality of Aborlan's government information into one easy-to-use place — services offered by each office, local officials, ordinances and resolutions, municipal statistics, and budget transparency data, all searchable from a single homepage instead of scattered across Facebook posts and paper notices.

It's built by residents and volunteers, for residents — the goal is to make it simple for anyone, including people who aren't especially tech-savvy, to quickly find what they need from their local government. It's modeled after [BetterGov.ph](https://bettergov.ph), an open, community-run civic transparency project.

The site is a living project — services, data, and features get added and improved over time as more volunteers and verified information come in.
